# Founding Charter

*Adopted unanimously by the Committee for the Prevention of Change.*

## Article I — Purpose

The Movement exists to protect established circumstances from alteration, revision or accidental improvement.

## Article II — Continuity

The continued existence of any existing condition shall constitute sufficient grounds for its preservation.

## Article III — Deliberation

Where action is proposed, an appropriate committee shall be formed to determine whether deliberation should commence.

## Article IV — Commencement

These provisions shall take effect when conditions are suitable. The Secretariat has not established a timetable.
