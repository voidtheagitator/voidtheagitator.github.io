# Membership

Membership is open, in principle, to any person committed to preserving current circumstances without introducing unnecessary complications.

## Obligations

Members are expected to demonstrate patience, restraint and an exceptional capacity for non-intervention.

## Applications

The Directorate is reviewing its application procedures. No formal applications are currently being accepted.

*An interactive application form may be introduced once the appropriate administrative approvals have been deferred.*
