# About the Movement

The Status Quo Immobilization Movement is a fictional institution devoted to the preservation of existing conditions, irrespective of their merit.

Its statements, investigations and publications form part of an ongoing satirical literary project. Nothing on this website should be interpreted as the work of a real political organisation, government department or public authority.

## Editorial scope

The Movement publishes absurdist short fiction, mock administrative documents, institutional correspondence and other material deemed unsuitable for meaningful intervention.
