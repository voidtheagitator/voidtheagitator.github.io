---
title: "Administrative Notice No. 001: The Establishment of an Office"
---
The Secretariat is pleased to announce that the Office of General Inaction has been formally constituted.

Following extensive preliminary discussion, it has been determined that the Office shall undertake a comprehensive assessment of the feasibility of performing no additional duties.

A report on the matter is expected at a date yet to be determined. Interested parties are advised not to make further enquiries.

**Issued by the Permanent Secretariat.**
