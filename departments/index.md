# Administrative Departments

The Movement conducts its affairs through a series of permanent, provisional and indefinitely pending committees.

## Office of General Inaction

Responsible for monitoring interventions that may otherwise have occurred.

## Committee for the Preservation of Existing Conditions

Assesses threats posed by reform, improvement and the passage of time.

## Department of Personal Affairs

Maintains confidential records of intimate matters with no obvious bearing on institutional operations.

## Secretariat for Unresolved Business

Accepts matters requiring urgent postponement.
